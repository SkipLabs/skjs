The producer proposes a classification for each call; Lean checks it and computes T.Observed and T.Result from it. Inside a body they stay abstract: T.Result ≤ N, and T.Observed resolves to T.Result.

Narrow lets T.Result be wider than needed: number classifies with result number | string. So T.Result is not unique; Awaited corresponds to the least.
