The relations Resolves and Observes

Rule

Resolves

Observes

stable

A ≤ N gives A ↦ A

A ≤ N gives A ↦ A

native

A ↦ R gives Promise<A> ↦ R

Promise<A> ↦ A

union

A ↦ R, B ↦ S gives A | B ↦ R | S

A ↦ O, B ↦ Q gives A | B ↦ O | Q

narrow

A ≤ B, B ↦ R gives A ↦ R

A ≤ B, B ↦ O gives A ↦ O

Relations between type meanings: resolution follows a native promise's payload until it reaches a stable type; observation reads it once. Narrow also admits wider results (number resolves to number | string); Awaited corresponds to the least.
