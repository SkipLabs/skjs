Exactness (a lemma): if X ≤ N, then X.Observed = X.Result = X. The three rules are the only ones, so a stable type has exactly one observation and one result.

Who decides. The OCaml producer proposes types only. Lean's proof search finds the classification of each call's type argument, and the Lean checker checks it. Inside a body the two types stay abstract: T.Result ≤ N, and T.Observed resolves to T.Result.

These are the inductive relations Observes and Resolves in ResolutionCapabilities.lean; the exactness lemmas are Observes.exact and Resolves.exact. A native promise is observable at any payload; its result needs the payload's result. A type with no classification, such as an open record, makes a call that needs its result refused.
