Definitions

Prim: the primitives. Obj: every other value.

Thenable: an object with a callable then.

Resolution: a primitive or non-thenable fulfills the promise; a thenable's then is called.

Promise<B>: a native promise whose fulfillment values lie in B.

N: values that can never have a callable then.

Calcagno · Typing Promise Resolution

4 / 14

Resolution follows ECMA-262, which looks up then only on objects; a thenable's then runs in a later job. A Promise may also reject or stay pending. N includes primitives, readonly arrays, and records whose type rules out then for their lifetime.
