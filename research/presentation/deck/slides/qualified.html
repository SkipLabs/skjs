  • T : Awaitable, written T extends Awaitable: T has an observation T.Observed, what await needs.
  • T : Resolvable, written T extends Resolvable: T also has a result T.Result, what a fresh resolution yields, never thenable; async return needs it. Awaited<T> in a signature implies it, and denotes T.Result.
  • Not a subtype bound. The values of T are unconstrained; each call supplies the classification of its type argument. Bounds are read off the signature.
