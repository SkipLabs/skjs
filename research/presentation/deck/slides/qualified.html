4. Generic Awaited<T>: qualified binders

Definition (qualified binder). A binder T used with Awaited comes with two companions: an observation O, what await yields, and a result R, what a fresh resolution yields.

A generic function means: it meets its signature at every assignment of types to (T, O, R) with

R ≤ N,   Resolves(T, R),   Observes(T, O),   Resolves(O, R)

Awaited<T> means R. Since R is never thenable, resolving it again changes nothing.
