What these annotations are

Every mark is one claim by the producer — a type it says holds. The verifier checks modeled function obligations; a function result does not establish every displayed claim.

╰─╯ under an expression: the claimed type of that expression. Two brackets can end at the same place because the spans nest — in typeof x the inner bracket is x and the outer one is typeof x, so they get their own rows.

╵ above a statement: the claimed types of the bindings in scope at that point. In Compact, environments show changes from the preceding entry in source order. This comparison does not describe control-flow edges. Literal restatements and nested expressions are hidden. Select an outer expression to inspect its nested claims, or choose Every claim.

╰─╯ in amber: a gap — somewhere the producer went but claimed nothing, or an edit against a baseline. Gaps show in both densities, because an absent claim is the finding. A dotted underline marks a recovery claim, from a recovery-quality collection snapshot.
